INVESTMENT OVERVIEW

CDAY aims to deliver attractive income while providing exposure primarily, but not exclusively, to Canadian equities. To supplement income, CDAY employs an actively managed, primarily ultra-short-term, option strategy on its North American equity exposure and employs modest 25% leverage.
