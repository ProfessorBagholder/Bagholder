## 1. The incremental read

How `from` is chosen. An account the book has a complete read of and that the broker states open goes into `to_read` with that read's instant (`rust/crates/broker/src/pull.rs:189-194`: `Some(t) if !a.open => closed.push((a, t))`, `last => to_read.push((a, last, None))`). `from` is the day of that instant in Wealthsimple's zone (`pull.rs:215`: `last.map(|t| adapter.day(t))`; `rust/crates/wealthsimple/src/adapter.rs:605-607`), lowered to the earliest day of a stored record of that account not yet final (`pull.rs:215-219`, from `unsettled` collected at `pull.rs:168-174`), and for a closed account lowered to the `since` day of what gave the reason (`pull.rs:220-221`). Nothing else lowers it.

What is sent. `Client::activity` builds `condition = {accountIds: [account]}` and adds `startDate` only when `from` is `Some` (`rust/crates/wealthsimple/src/client.rs:235-240`); `startDate` is midnight of that day in `ZONE` as an RFC-3339 instant (`client.rs:219-224`; `ZONE` is `America/Toronto`, `rust/crates/wealthsimple/src/mapping.rs:54`). Pages are `first: 100` with a cursor until `hasNextPage` is false (`client.rs:241`, `client.rs:194-212`). No `orderBy`, `accountScope` or type/status condition is sent, so the document's defaults apply.

What `startDate` filters on. The document declares `$condition: ActivityCondition` and names no field of it (`rust/crates/wealthsimple/graphql/FetchActivityFeedItems.graphql:2`); the only date-like names in the document are the ordering default `OCCURRED_AT_DESC` (line 2) and the row field `occurredAt` (line 32). No recorded reply carries a `startDate` or a `condition` (grep of `rust/crates/wealthsimple/tests/replies/`: none). So whether Wealthsimple applies `startDate` to `occurredAt` or to some created/posted time: not found. What the code assumes: the adapter dates every row by `occurredAt` (`adapter.rs:102-105`), the replay used in tests answers `activity(account, from)` by filtering rows whose `occurredAt` day is `>= from` (`rust/crates/wealthsimple/src/replay.rs:191-194`), and the plan records the settled rule as "the feed filters by account, dates, types and status, never by when a row changed" (`docs/plans/stage-3b-wealthsimple.md:63`).

Conclusion. Under the code's own reading (and the plan's), a row Wealthsimple adds later whose `occurredAt` falls before the last complete read's day is not asked for by an incremental read, open account or closed alike: `from` is lowered only by an unsettled record of that account (`pull.rs:215-219`) or, for a closed account, a `closed_due` reason (`pull.rs:220-221`, `pull.rs:557-597`). The plan says such a row "is found by the broker check, not by re-reading: an account whose cash or units differ ... is re-read over the span the difference points to" (`docs/plans/stage-3b-wealthsimple.md:63`); that re-read does not exist in code: `pull.rs` has no path from a broker-check difference to an activity read (no `Difference` or `BrokerCheck` in `rust/crates/broker/src`), and the only automatic trigger from the balances, `cash_moved` (`pull.rs:894-897`, `pull.rs:929-938`), starts `pull_activity` (`rust/crates/server/src/broker_reads.rs:303`, `:385`, `:394-397`, `broker_reads.rs:512`), which reads from the last full read again (`pull.rs:94-101`). If Wealthsimple instead filtered on a posting time, a late row would be caught, but then `unlisted`'s span logic, which takes a read from day D to cover every stored row whose `occurredAt` day is `>= D` (`pull.rs:623`, `pull.rs:636`), would be wrong the other way; the repository cannot tell which.

## 2. A full re-read of one account (from = None)

When it happens today. Only when the book has no complete read of the account (`pull.rs:190-193`: `last` is `None`; `pull.rs:215`), or a stored record of it is not final and its day is unknown (`pull.rs:218`: `Some(None) => None`). A read is complete only when no row lacked an id (`pull.rs:226-228`, `pull.rs:351-353`); `activity_read_at` is the newest complete read (`rust/crates/book/src/statements.rs:535-538`, `complete = 1`), and reads are keyed by `(account_id, read_at)` so a later incomplete read does not replace a complete one (`rust/crates/book/migrations/006-the-broker-s-statements.sql:69-74`).

What happens to rows no longer listed. `spans[account] = None` (`pull.rs:228`), and `covers` is then true for every day (`pull.rs:623`). `unlisted` (`pull.rs:613-679`) gathers every live `wealthsimple` record of that account whose key is not among the rows read (`pull.rs:628-640`, keyed by `adapter.placed`, `adapter.rs:601-604`). The guard (`pull.rs:641-655`): if any final row gone is dated before `read_before` (the day of the last complete read, set at `pull.rs:212-214`) the account is reported `suspect` and nothing of it is removed (`pull.rs:645-647`, `pull.rs:652`); if more than `FEW = 3` final rows are gone, likewise (`pull.rs:547`, `pull.rs:648`); otherwise every gone row, final or not, is `mark_removed` (`pull.rs:653`, `pull.rs:347-350`; `rust/crates/book/src/records.rs:471`). So for an account with a prior complete read, a full re-read that no longer lists any final row from before that day removes nothing for the account; it can remove at most three final rows dated on or after that day, plus any number of non-final rows. For an account never read in full, `read_before` is unset so only the count guard applies (`pull.rs:645` is false). Tests of the guard: `rust/crates/wealthsimple/tests/pull.rs:351` and `tests/pull.rs:370`.

Imported records on a full re-read. Live records of another source keyed by `broker-record:wealthsimple` whose key is not listed, and whose transactions' accounts all have a span covering their first day, are removed with no guard (`pull.rs:667-677`; the doc comment says why at `pull.rs:609-612`). With `spans = None` that is every such import of the account not listed now. Imports whose row is listed are superseded by the row's own record instead (`pull.rs:699-713`, `rust/crates/book/src/records.rs:132`). The scheme is the earlier app's import mapping's (`rust/crates/book/src/import/mapping.rs:35`); the CSV file import does not use it (it links by supersede in `rust/crates/server/src/csv_import.rs:476-518`).

Whether a full re-read damages anything else. A row whose stored `activity` object equals the row as read is counted unchanged and not put together or written again (`pull.rs:250-256`, `held` at `pull.rs:550-555`, `adapter.rs:426-428`). A row whose JSON differs is re-assembled and stored as a revision (`pull.rs:690-718`). The read is marked complete at the end (`pull.rs:351-353`). Beyond the removals above, nothing stored is overwritten.

Cost. One `FetchActivityFeedItems` request per 100 rows (`client.rs:241`) until `hasNextPage` is false (`client.rs:198-211`): 2,000 rows is 20 requests, at no less than 150 ms apart (`client.rs:18`, `client.rs:100-105`), so about three seconds for the feed alone. Rows the book already holds unchanged cost nothing more. A changed row costs its own reads: securities in batches of 50 (`adapter.rs:115-132`, `adapter.rs:421-425`) and, per row, whatever `needs` names (`rust/crates/wealthsimple/src/assemble.rs:111-161`, `assemble.rs:186-336`).

Statements. `statement_keys` are the broker accounts behind each book account in `keys_of`, closed ones included when they sit behind an open book account (`pull.rs:377-385`, comment `pull.rs:374-376`). [corrected] So the rule is per open book account, not per open broker account: a closed broker account merged into an open book account is read for statements, while a closed account that is its own book account is not. `statements::run` reads only an account whose `differing` set (book cash per currency versus stated cash) is non-empty (`rust/crates/broker/src/statements.rs:153-157`, `statements.rs:565-580`), and a closed broker account with no statement rows is taken to hold zero in `closings` (`statements.rs:921-941`).

Units and history. Units are read for `keys_of` only, on a daily pull (`pull.rs:393-407`, as of yesterday `pull.rs:388`); history skips a closed account once it has any day stored (`pull.rs:489-494`).

A closed account today. Its activity is read only when `closed_due` finds a reason (`pull.rs:191`, `pull.rs:197-207`, `pull.rs:557-597`); it gets no cash, no net value, no units, and no further history.
