# Brief 28: verdict on the deep-history plan (#511, `plan-deep-history` at `ed0a5666`), and brief 27's fixes as merged: Go with changes

**Reviewed:** `docs/plans/stage-3-a-change-on-a-deep-history.md` against master `e340e0d7` (2.5.6, which carries #505, #506 and #507). Read: `book/src/{identity.rs (names, current_symbol, record_sighting, settle_successions), lib.rs (atomically, snapshot), records.rs, links.rs, clear.rs, changes.rs}`, `sqlite/src/lib.rs`, migration 028, `engine/src/{ledger.rs (Matcher, match_over, the look-ahead, setup, expire_before), families.rs, engine.rs (rematch_families, rederive, Change::Rates), identity.rs, positions.rs, trades.rs, cashflow.rs, dust.rs}`, `server/src/{figures.rs, capacity.rs, entries.rs, csv_import.rs}`, `engine/tests/by_change.rs`. Three readers took the plan's parts A, B and C with the gate; a fourth checked brief 27's thirteen fixes as merged, running tests on a throwaway copy with each fix undone in turn. I checked every finding below against the code myself. The plan's three cited sources were re-read 2026-10-13 and say what it quotes: ERPNext's repost of "every subsequent stock ledger entry for that item-warehouse", NetSuite's per-item recalculation, and Axon's snapshot "at a specific position in the event stream".

## Verdict

Go with changes. The three steps are the standard ones and in the right order. Names are kept as rows, with the fold kept as the oracle. The family's match continues from a snapshot, with the whole match as the fallback. Figures are rebuilt per round trip. But each part, as written, would leave a figure or a name different from a fresh build. A misses two kinds of write. B's guards check one wrong field and miss three pieces of state. C's narrowing misses positions, identity by fill, rolls and groups. And the oracles and counters the plan relies on would not run in the tests that are meant to show them. Fifteen changes.

## Required changes

1. **Every change to a sighting must leave a due row, including UPDATE and REPLACE.** The plan triggers on insert and delete only (line 71). `record_sighting` writes `INSERT OR REPLACE INTO instrument_sightings` (`identity.rs:939`). No connection sets `recursive_triggers`, so a REPLACE fires no DELETE trigger. And `figures.rs:783`, an existing test, renames a sighting with a raw `UPDATE`. Under the plan that test's assertion "the head kept its old name" fails. `same_as_fresh` does not catch it, because a fresh build reads the same stale runs. Required: UPDATE triggers logging the OLD and NEW keys, on sightings and on `instrument_joins` and `source_records.state` too; `record_sighting` as `ON CONFLICT DO UPDATE`; the property test writing UPDATEs outside `atomically`.

2. **The continued match must keep the instant's holdings across continued walks, narrowed to what the look-ahead reads.** The close-before-open look-ahead (`ledger.rs:2255–2260`) fires only from a stated Open row and only takes single-leg stated Close rows on the same holding. Scenario, all at instant I with 1 short of contract C held: step 1 adds a stated-Open buy of C, which goes unapplied; step 2 adds a buy of the underlying, so the kept holdings re-form as {underlying}; step 3 adds a stated-Close buy of C, which continues and closes the short. A whole match takes the close first, so the open opens a long round trip. Required: the kept set is the union over walks at the same instant, reset when the instant changes, and it holds only the holdings of walked stated-Open rows. Narrowing it this way also lets the probe's ordinary case continue: hand-entered trades are untimed with no effect (`person.rs:204–208`).

3. **A trade or journal change must rebuild the position that shows the trade.** A position carries its first lot's trade and that trade's journal (`positions.rs:235–236`). Under C a trade change reaches only its round trip (line 113) and positions are rebuilt only for holdings walked (line 120). This is the probe's own second step: the added trade opens a round trip, and `settle_trades` then opens its trade, a trade change that walks no holding. The position keeps `trade: None` where a fresh build has the trade. Required: on a trade or journal change, rebuild every position whose first-lot round trip is reached, before and after.

4. **Identity must be rebuilt from trades anchored on any fill, not from an opening index.** A trade anchored on a fill that is not an opening is moved to the one round trip holding that fill, if it has no trade yet (`identity.rs:115–117`). An opening-to-trade index (line 117) cannot see that. Scenario: trade T is anchored on an add-on buy of round trip K; a continued sale writes K; the index finds no trade, so K lands in `needs_trade` while a fresh build has K's trade as T. A removed trade frees K for a fill-anchored trade the change never names. Required: index anchor transaction → trades and transaction → round trips; `identify_trips` takes every trade anchored on any fill of a reached round trip, and `Identity::replace` updates `moved` and `unclaimed` for those.

5. **Guard 6 must compare what the match reads, and only for accounts that held the family's rows.** The match reads an account's `coin_minimum` (`dust.rs:32`), not its type. A smallest coin order raised in the same change as an added coin trade passes guard 6, and an earlier remainder that is now dust stays open. And both probes create the Manual account inside their window (`entries.rs:138`, `csv_import.rs:168`); its insert is logged, so a literal guard 6 sends the probes whole and trips the plan's own right to refuse. Required: compare the instrument's kind, currency and terms, and each account's `coin_minimum`, for accounts that held family rows before the change. Add "a first trade into a new account" as a step that continues.

6. **The setup `match_over` works out from every row must be extended with the added rows.** `links_out` and `links_in`, `places`, `ended_on_record` and `event_groups` are built up front from all the family's rows (`ledger.rs:2196–2217`). Scenario: an import adds a transfer out and its linked transfer in, both new. Guard 4 passes, but with only the kept links the receiving side opens a lot at no known cost instead of taking the sent lots. A first hand-entered row on a coin is a new source with no `places` entry, so the rounding allowance differs from a whole match. Required: before the continued walk, run the setup over the added rows and merge it into the kept state; assert that `ended_on_record` and `event_groups` gain nothing; make guard 5 per (instrument, source); add "a linked pair, both added" as a continued step.

7. **A rolled round trip spans two holdings, and both must be rebuilt.** A roll joins the new contract's opening to the old round trip (`ledger.rs:1554`, :893–894). A position's fills come from every lot's round trip (`positions.rs:239–241`). A later continued sale of the new contract writes the round trip, but the old contract's position, which also shows it, is not rebuilt. Required: rebuild positions for every holding with lots of a written round trip; add a partial roll to the generator.

8. **Due rows must be checked and settled by head, and `current_symbol` checked too.** Sightings are kept under the member's own id (`identity.rs:562`, :609), so a due row holds the member, while the plan checks and settles by head (lines 75, 85). The check finds nothing, so the stale runs are read; if settle walks only heads, member rows are never cleared, and that head folds on every read. `current_symbol` (line 87) gets no due check at all. And the plan's "the person's chosen listing" is not one of `current_symbol`'s fallbacks on master (`identity.rs:426–430`). Required: check `names_due` over the head's whole group; settle resolves each due row to its current head; drop runs kept under ids that are no longer heads; give `current_symbol` the same check. Give `names_due` the three change-log triggers, or state its exemption in the authorizer test.

9. **Clear must neither fail nor erase names a partial clear keeps.** If the new tables reference `instruments`, the sighting deletes in `Book::clear` queue due rows for instruments it then deletes (`clear.rs:171`, :255), and the clear fails on a foreign key. If both tables are simply emptied (line 150), a Clear of entries alone (`clear.rs:144`) leaves broker-held listings with sightings but no runs and no due rows: no name, and `Book::name` refuses to enter a trade of them (`person.rs:280`). Required: no foreign keys on the two tables, and let the triggers and the settle at the end of Clear's own transaction maintain them; classify `names_due` for the clear-all test.

10. **The oracles and the counter must exist where the tests run.** `#[cfg(test)]` in the `book` and `engine` crates compiles only for those crates' own unit tests. `book/tests/*.rs`, `engine/tests/by_change.rs` and every server test link the libraries without it, so the names oracle (line 85), the continued-match oracle (line 107) and the counter (line 140) would not exist in the tests that cite them, and the criteria would pass vacuously. Required: a dev-only cargo feature, as `book` already does with `pinned-tzdb`, enabled from each consumer's dev-dependencies and off inside a probe's window. The counter's expected path comes from the generator's own labels per family per step, never from asking the guard, with a minimum number of continued steps asserted.

11. **A change of exchange rates must not leave a kept state stale.** Dust is judged at the row's day's rate (`dust.rs:38`), and while a rate is pending the remainder is kept. `Change::Rates` derives but does not re-match (`engine.rs:671–676`). Today the next trade's whole-family match heals that; a continued match would keep the stale remainder for good. Required: on `Change::Rates`, re-match the coin families from the first day that changed, or drop their kept state. The gap already exists on master between a rates change and the next trade: file it as an issue, closed by this build.

12. **Groups are recomputed when a trade the change names is a member.** `groups_shown` claims round trips through each member's `trip_of`, in book order (`trades.rs:344`). Reattaching an orphaned member to a round trip that no group holds, or a removed trade letting a member move, changes a group's claims while "a round trip reached is held by a group" (line 119) is false. Required: also recompute groups when any trade the change names, before or after, is a member.

13. **Wiring must not wipe a continued family.** `rematch_families` replaces everything keyed by the region and drops the shares of every family in `gone` (`engine.rs:397`, :401), and `regroup` puts every family of the region in `gone` even when it comes back unchanged (`families.rs:272–276`). Required: compare members before and after to tell "unchanged"; pass `replace` only the families matched whole; keep the shares of continued ones; drop the kept state of families that went.

14. **The in-suite growth test must be deep enough to fail.** It compares deep 20 with deep 80 (`capacity.rs:1090`, rows 39 and 159), four times, not the sixteen the plan says (line 144). At about 29 steps a row, a planted whole fold adds about 1.45×, under the 2× limit, so the plant would not fail it. Required: state `deep: 320` (rows 639) in D, and assert the continued counter ran for the probe.

15. **Smaller, each to be stated in the plan:**
    - Settle each head under its own savepoint. A corrupt row would otherwise fail every write and every `Book::open`, Clear included, where on master it fails one instrument's names.
    - `Book::atomically` returns early when a transaction is already open (`lib.rs:124`), so a write inside `bagholder_sqlite::atomically` or `snapshot` commits due rows unsettled. Assert in test builds that nothing is due after a commit.
    - "After" is `order_key` strictly greater than the largest walked, without the transaction id; the walk breaks ties by ledger place.
    - `regroup` still reads every row of the family (`families.rs:194`), CPU the gate cannot see. Decide guard 3 from the added rows, or name the leftover.

## Brief 27's fixes, as merged

#507 merged with the fixes before this review; they were checked now. All thirteen are in the code. Fixes 6 and 13 were done differently, and as well. Fixes 9 and 10 are partly done, and several tests hold less than they claim. Six defects remain, to go in this plan's PR or one beside it:

- **The in-suite growth test still cannot catch one whole read in the import** (fix 9). It filters out bytes read (`capacity.rs:1103`), and the plant is the whole linking pass. With one `live_records(csv)` read planted instead, steps grew 1.75× and only bytes read fired, which is ignored, so the plant went unseen. The PR's sentence that the test "fails if either grows with the book" is not true as built. Plant exactly that read and make the test catch it.
- **Fix 1's test holds only its name step.** Its ref step adds a second broker id beside one the head already has, so nothing shown changes; its removal step is logged by record and never needed the fix. Delete the existing refs first, and store a record under the member.
- **Names are still lost if a pull fails after its first catch-up** (fix 5). `catch_up` applies, moves the watermark and trims the log (`figures.rs:581–586`); an error in `settle_trades` or `book.stated` returns before the reload at :595. On any error after the first catch-up, reload the gathered names or reset the cache. Its test calls `catch_up` directly and never goes through `pulled`.
- **The names cache can be written stale** (fix 12). `names()` loads under the engine's read lock and writes the cache after releasing it (`figures.rs:471–472`); an install or a `pulled` landing between the two is overwritten. Keep the read lock across the write.
- **The race test does not race the read-back** (fix 13). Its hook fires only in the catch-up the test calls itself, so `settle_trades`' stale-anchor guards are never raced against `fills_give_way`.
- **The server's fresh-build check does not compare order or the match** (fix 11). `same_as_fresh` (`figures.rs:728–742`) compares by key and never calls `match_disagrees`, so no server-level test compares shown order, the match or identity. Add it.

Fix 2 is held by only 1 of the 60 generated books; widen the generator until several catch it. Fix 7 has no test.

## Accepted as is

Names as rows with the fold as the oracle, and reads falling back to the fold while anything is due. Due rows settled before the outermost commit. Every production writer of sightings, record state and joins is inside `Book::atomically`. The re-forming around due sightings equals the fold once every change leaves a due row, with venue compared with `IS`. The match continued from a snapshot taken before the final `expire_before(today)`, with the whole family as the fallback. Guards 1, 3, 4 and 7 as written. Guard 2's "every leg", which covers legs in other families. Lists put in place with `partition_point`, which equals the stable sort for new ids. Units recorded per holding touched. Cash shares by exact addition. `Engine::past` matching afresh. A round trip's figure reads only its own round trip, so an unwritten one cannot change. Broker checks for every account holding a reached instrument. Payers by an index over every account's rows. Back-dated rows still going whole, under #512, as ERPNext's "every subsequent" entry recompute would place it. The build order A, measured alone, then B, C, D.
